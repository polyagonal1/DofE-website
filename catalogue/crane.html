--- layout: instruction_page short_title: Origami Crane title: Making an Origami Crane perma_id: making_crane thumbnail: sources: [] img: src: thumbnail/thumbnail.jpg alt: Picture of a completed origami crane img_folder: crane/ steps_folder: steps/ steps: - Fold the paper in half horizontally, then unfold. - Fold the paper in half vertically, then unfold. - Fold the paper in half diagonally from the top left corner to the bottom right corner, then unfold. - Fold the paper in half diagonally from the top right corner to the bottom left corner to form a triangle, but don't unfold. - Fold the paper in half again to form a smaller triangle. - Bring up the top flap and open it up and squash it down flat (squash fold). Do the same on the other side. Since you have already creased the places we are folding this should be easy to do. - Fold in the top flaps on each side of the square to the middle and unfold. Repeat on the other side. - Fold in the top triangle shape in on top of where you folded in the previous step and unfold. - Open the flap on top and petal fold it upwards on the lines shown. Repeat on the other side. - Fold the top flaps in to the middle. Repeat on the other side. - Reverse fold at the top of each of the object's arms. These will form the tail and the head. - Reverse fold at the top of one of the object's arms. This is the head. - Finish off by curling the wings down and gently expanding the crane's back. - description: And that is it.
